# Project One Storyboard | Text-Based Adventure Game

## Theme and Storyline

**Theme:**
The theme of my game is a Warehouse Mystery. The game takes place during the night shift inside a large warehouse where valuable shipments have mysteriously disappeared. The player must explore different areas of the warehouse, collect evidence, and avoid the villain.

**Storyline:**
The player is working the night shift when they discover that several expensive shipments are missing. While investigating, the player realizes that the person responsible is still somewhere inside the warehouse. The player must travel through the warehouse and collect six pieces of evidence that will reveal who stole the shipments. The player must collect all six items before entering the Manager's Office, where the Warehouse Thief is hiding. If the player collects all six pieces of evidence before encountering the thief, the player wins the game. If the player encounters the Warehouse Thief before collecting all six items, the player loses.

## Villain

The villain is the **Warehouse Thief**, who is hiding in the **Manager's Office**. The player must avoid the Warehouse Thief until all six pieces of evidence have been collected. If the player enters the Manager's Office before collecting all six items, the player loses the game.
